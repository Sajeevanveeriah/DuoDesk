A few practical details.

What needs permission?

On macOS, screen capture and input need the relevant Privacy & Security permissions. Android notifications, texts, camera and control need their own permissions or wireless debugging. A feature being present in source does not mean it works on every device without setup.

Does Bluetooth carry everything?

No. Bluetooth LE supports the control connection, while video and larger transfers may use Wi-Fi Direct, a hotspot or a USB path. Calls' sound requires classic Bluetooth pairing.

Is this a verified release?

The status above is read from public GitHub releases. Mock-window checks and real-device testing are separate. Review the release notes for what has been tested on actual hardware.

What does this website count?

When configured, this site counts page views and download redirects by day and operating system. It stores no cookies, IP addresses or visitor identifiers in its analytics table. Counts are events, not unique people or confirmed installations. Hosting providers may keep their own operational logs.
